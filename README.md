# Why I Chose to Build My Own
The reason behind why I did not buy a solder fume extractor online is because the ones online are easily over $50 and there were not much information on how to buy a good one.
Upon research, I discovered that the best way is just to vent the air completely out the window.
That is why I initially only had the inline duct fan and the duct pipe to carry the solder fume outdoor.
However, I felt like blowing the solder fume without filtering it would harm the neighbors.
Many solder fume extractors sold on the internet uses carbon filters, but I find them unreliable as I can easily see through the filter and the carbon powder on them are too little.
I wanted to find a filter that can capture both particulate matter and volatile organic compounds (VOC).
While the filter I selected might not be the best, Jafanda filter has both HEPA filter, which captures the particulate matter, and activated carbon filter, which captures the VOCs.
If you would like to use another filter, I recommend one with both HEPA and activated carbon filter.
